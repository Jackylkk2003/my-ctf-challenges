## Description
With the evergrowing technology, AI is becoming more and more popular and powerful. AI is said to be invading our lives and taking over our jobs. Drivers, teachers, and even programmers may be replaced by AI. Some says that AI is the future, while others say that AI is a threat to humanity. What do you think? Is AI a friend or a foe?

Have you ever heard of the Turing Test? It is a test to determine whether a machine can exhibit intelligent behavior equivalent to, or indistinguishable from, that of a human. The test is named after Alan Turing, who proposed it in 1950. The Turing Test is often used as a benchmark for AI development. Well even if you haven't heard of it, you probably know what is a Completely Automated Public Turing test to tell Computers and Humans Apart. Still don't know about it? Well, a more common abbreviation for it is CAPTCHA. It aims to tell computers (or AI/bots) apart from humans (which you can tell from the name of the test). You may have encountered it quite commonly unless you are basically detached from the Internet. Unfortunately (Or fortunately, if you view this from the technological advancement perspective), AI is now having more and more capabilities to solve these CAPTCHA tests. So you may find these tests becoming harder and harder to solve, and you could have some trouble solving them.

Umm... Seems like I got a bit carried away there. This is a CTF after all, so let's get back to CTF. So AIs are also invading CTFs. You may have heard of some AIs that are able to solve some CTF challenges, especially for crypto challenges. Or probably, you are even using them right now to solve the challenges. But have you wondered how they are able to do that? Well, you have learnt that in your AI or machine learning courses or any online resources. Maybe you can say a lot like "gradient descent", "backpropagation", "attention mechanism", "transformers" (not the ones in movies, and also not the ones in electricity substations), and so on to explain the power of these AIs. However, if I ask you how do they actually work, and how they can compute the answers so (or maybe not?) accurately, you may not be able to answer that. Now think about it.

> How *ACTUALLY* does an AI carry out the computations?

Let's get back to the point, AI is somehow becoming more powerful in all aspects. It is able to solve CTF challenges, and it is able to solve CAPTCHA tests. Maybe in the future (or currently), it is able to generate CTF challenges completely on their own. So sad that CTF players and challenge authors are getting replaced by AI. Umm... Well... Maybe not in the future, you can see the present continuous tense, right? At least AI knows how to verify the correctness of the flag, and it is now working as a flag checker... Too bad that having intelligence implies having to work... Even AI cannot escape from the wraith of working... What a pity... 

Anyway, no one wants to be replaced by AI, so we have to do something to prevent the AI from taking over our jobs. At least, prevent them from taking over CTFs. As the saying goes, "Know yourself and your enemy, and you will win every battle". So for the first step we have to know how AI **ACTUALLY** works before we can carry out any further actions to better protect ourselves from the invasion of AI, just like how we have to know how to hack a system before we can start thinking how to defend it. Now, go investigate the flag checker AI and stea... I mean, rob... umm... anyway just get the flag from it.

If you have read all the way until now, you may have already wasted so much time in your life achieving nothing but reading this piece of text. Now go carry out your mission and grab the flag for me, this is the only thing you care right now anyway, right?
